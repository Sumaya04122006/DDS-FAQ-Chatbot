# How to Participate

1. **Sign up:** click https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/challenges/building-agentic-ai-application-nov on the challenge page to register (free).
2. **Follow the checkpoints:** you receive daily instructions and guidance on the platform.
3. **Submit your work:** submit progress **every other day**, then a **final submission and a LinkedIn post**.
4. **Win:** stand a chance at scholarships, licences, swag and internships. Winners are announced at the community meetup.

## Mandatory actions (Day 0)
- Watch the updated Day 0 video (must watch).
- Join the **WhatsApp group** and introduce yourself.
- Download the **Project Idea and Planning Template**.
- Complete the **AI Builder Codex**: https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/courses/the-dds-builder-codex
- Join the **DDS Private Community** (required for judges to review your work).

## What a strong final submission contains (practical checklist)
- [ ] Working app with a **public link** (hosted demo)
- [ ] **GitHub repository** link (public, or access for judges) with a clear README
- [ ] Short **demo video** (1–3 min) – problem, solution, live walkthrough
- [ ] One-paragraph description: user, problem, what makes it agentic, tools/models used
- [ ] Screenshots
- [ ] **LinkedIn post** (tag DDS, include the demo link and the hashtags used by the challenge)
- [ ] Submitted **before 15 Nov 2026, 11:45 PM GST**
Always follow the exact fields of the challenge platform's final checkpoint; the list above is general good practice.

## Optional bonus points (up to 250 points)
- **Post on LinkedIn announcing you are joining the challenge → 150 bonus points.**
  - Share it in the LinkedIn group: https://linkedin.com/groups/9371112/
  - Tag DDS: https://linkedin.com/company/dds-business-circle/
  - Use the announcement poster provided.
- **Post on other platforms:** tag DDS on Instagram (https://instagram.com/decodingdatascience/) and on X (https://x.com/decodingdatasci).
- **Comment your excitement** on the pinned LinkedIn post (link in the challenge materials).
- **Post a community update on Skool:** https://skool.com/decoding-data-science-6929/
The materials say bonus points total **up to 250**. The exact points for each individual action other than the 150-point LinkedIn announcement are not itemised publicly.

## Late or problem submissions
If the platform fails or you have an emergency, contact the organisers immediately via the WhatsApp group or connect@decodingdatascience.com **before** the deadline. Do not assume exceptions will be made.
