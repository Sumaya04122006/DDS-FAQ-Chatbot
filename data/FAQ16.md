# FAQ

## About the challenge
**Q: What is the challenge?**
A free, remote 8-day challenge by Decoding Data Science to build, deploy and showcase a real agentic AI application.

**Q: When does it start and end?**
It starts **7 November 2026, 9:00 AM GST**. Final submissions close **15 November 2026, 11:45 PM GST** (GST = UTC+4).

**Q: Is it free?**
Yes, the challenge is free to join (for a limited time). Support materials and recordings are optional and paid or discounted.

**Q: Do I need coding experience?**
No. The No-Code/Low-Code path needs no coding. The Code-First path benefits from basic Python.

**Q: Can I use AI coding tools?**
Yes. Cursor, Claude Code, Codex and similar tools are explicitly welcomed. You don't need to write every line manually.

**Q: Which tools am I allowed to use?**
Named examples: Python, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, OpenAI APIs, Amazon Bedrock (code-first); Lovable, Base44, n8n, Flowise, Make (no-code/low-code). Other tools are fine as long as your final project is a working AI application.

**Q: Can I work in a team?**
The materials describe individual participation and individual awards. If you want to team up, confirm with the organisers first.

**Q: Is it only for people in the UAE?**
The challenge is remote and open to a global audience. Winners are announced at a community meetup.

## Before you start (Day 0)
**Q: What must I do before the start?**
Watch the Day 0 video, join the WhatsApp group and introduce yourself, download the Project Idea and Planning Template, complete the AI Builder Codex, and submit the Day 0 checkpoint. Also join the DDS Private Community (needed for judges to review your work).

**Q: What do I get for finishing Day 0 early?**
Completing all Day 0 checkpoints before the challenge starts gives you the AI Builder's Support Kit for free.

## During the challenge
**Q: How often do I submit?**
Every other day during Days 2–7, plus the final submission and LinkedIn post on Day 8.

**Q: What is the final deadline?**
15 Nov 2026, 11:45 PM GST. Submit hours early.

**Q: What should my final submission include?**
A working demo link, repository link, short demo video, description, and your LinkedIn post. Follow the exact fields on the platform.

**Q: Do I have to post on LinkedIn?**
The final submission includes a LinkedIn post, and "Presentation" scoring covers the quality of the demo and LinkedIn post. A LinkedIn announcement post at the start also earns 150 bonus points.

**Q: What if I can't finish?**
Submit what works. A small working app beats a large broken one. Deploy and document what you have.

## Technical questions
**Q: What is agentic AI?** See the "What Is Agentic AI" document. In short: AI that plans, uses tools and takes multi-step actions toward a goal.

**Q: Is a RAG chatbot enough to count as agentic?**
A RAG chatbot is a good foundation but, on its own, it is closer to a retrieval-augmented assistant. To be agentic, add decision-making and tools: e.g., the agent decides when to search, calls extra tools (APIs, calculators, databases), keeps state and takes actions with guardrails.

**Q: Which LLM should I use?**
Pick one you can access reliably (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Amazon Bedrock, or an open model via a hosted API). Check rate limits and costs early.

**Q: Where can I host for free?**
Hugging Face Spaces, Render, Vercel (front-end), Streamlit Community Cloud, Railway (trial/credits), Supabase (database). See the Free Hosting guide for limits like sleeping and ephemeral disks.

**Q: My app is slow on the first load.**
Free hosts sleep when idle and need time to wake. Open it before demos and mention it in the README.

**Q: My deployment fails.**
Check build/runtime logs; usual causes are missing packages, wrong start command/port, or missing environment variables. See Troubleshooting.

**Q: How do I keep my API key safe?**
Environment variables and host secrets only; never commit it; rotate if leaked.

**Q: Do I need to pay for APIs?**
Possibly. Some providers have free tiers with limits; others are pay-as-you-go. Set budgets and use small models while testing.
