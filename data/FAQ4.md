# Judging Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation | **25%** | Originality and creativity of the application |
| Technical Execution | **25%** | Quality of code and AI model implementation |
| Real-World Applicability | **15%** | Practicality and impact of the application |
| Presentation | **15%** | Quality of the demo and the LinkedIn post |
| Platform Leaderboard | **20%** | Timely submissions and engagement on the platform |
Total = 100%.

## What scores well (practical guidance)
**Innovation (25%)**
- A fresh angle on a real problem beats a copy of a common tutorial chatbot.
- A clear "why agentic": the system plans, uses tools, takes actions, not just answers questions.

**Technical Execution (25%)**
- App works end to end for a judge who has never seen it.
- Sensible architecture, error handling, secrets kept out of the repository, clear README.
- For no-code: clean workflow, tested edge cases, sensible prompts, reliable tool calls.

**Real-World Applicability (15%)**
- A named user, a named problem, and evidence it helps (even a small test with 3 people).

**Presentation (15%)**
- A short demo video (1–3 minutes) with a clear story: problem, solution, live demo, result.
- A LinkedIn post that explains what you built, who it helps and includes the demo link.

**Platform Leaderboard (20%)**
- Submit **on time** at every checkpoint (every other day).
- Engage with the community: comments, community update posts, helping others.
- This is the easiest 20% to lose by being late.
