# Prizes, Rewards and Awards (as published for the November 2026 edition)

## Headline totals
- Banner: "Win up to **$10,000** scholarships & license fees. Supported by DDS, JetBrains."
- How-to-participate text: "$6,900 scholarship and $5,000 Licenses & Swags, Internships, and more."
These two headline phrasings are both used in official materials. The itemised programme prizes below are the reliable detail.

## JetBrains licences (sponsor)
**JetBrains All Products Pack licences: 5 winners in total.** Listed value: **$1,027 each** in the main prize table.
- **Top 3 – Best Projects (3 licences):** 1st Place – Best Project; 2nd Place – Runner-Up; 3rd Place – Third Place.
- **Special Categories (2 licences):**
  - **Women in AI Award:** best project by a woman participant.
  - **Rising Star (Student) Award:** best project by a DDS student participant (high potential and strong execution).
License awards are supported by JetBrains.
Note: one slide lists the two special-category licences as "worth $770". If a participant asks about exact licence value, quote the published figures and suggest confirming with connect@decodingdatascience.com.
