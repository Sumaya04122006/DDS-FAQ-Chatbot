# Timeline, Checkpoints and Deadlines (Building Agentic AI Application Challenge, November 2026)

## Hard dates
| Event | Date / time |
|---|---|
| Day 0 (mandatory, before start) | Complete **before the challenge starts** |
| Challenge start | **7 November 2026, 9:00 AM GST** |
| Day 1: Ideation and Conceptualization | **7 Nov – 8 Nov** |
| Day 8: Final Submission and LinkedIn Sharing | **14 Nov – 15 Nov** |
| Final submission deadline | **15 November 2026, 11:45 PM GST** |

GST = Gulf Standard Time = UTC+4 (the UAE does not use daylight saving time). To convert: 9:00 AM GST is 5:00 AM UTC; 11:45 PM GST is 7:45 PM UTC.

## Structure
- The challenge has **9 checkpoints** on the challenge platform (Day 0 through Day 8).
- **Day 1:** Kickoff and project ideation. You choose your path (Code-First or No-Code/Low-Code) on Day 1.
- **Day 2–7:** Daily checkpoints, with **submissions every other day**.
- **Day 8:** Final submission and LinkedIn sharing.
- The exact open/close time of each intermediate checkpoint is shown on the challenge platform. Always trust the platform's checkpoint page for the exact times.

## Day 0 – Mandatory actions (before start)
Day 0 is titled "Mandatory Watch and Submission Before Start". It includes:
1. Watch the updated Day 0 video recorded for this challenge (**must watch**).
2. Join the **WhatsApp group** and introduce yourself.
3. Download the official **Project Idea and Planning Template**; use it to clarify your idea, define your audience and map your approach.
4. Complete the **AI Builder Codex** course: https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/courses/the-dds-builder-codex
5. Submit the Day 0 checkpoint.
Completing all Day 0 checkpoints before the challenge starts also unlocks the **AI Builder's Support Kit for free** (see Support Materials).

## Recommended 8-day build plan (guidance, not an official schedule)
- **Day 0:** Finish mandatory actions. Choose an idea on paper. Create GitHub and hosting accounts.
- **Day 1 (7–8 Nov):** Lock the idea, user, problem, input and expected output. Pick your path and tools. Create the repository.
- **Day 2–3:** Build the smallest working version (one clear problem solved end to end). Submit the checkpoint.
- **Day 4–5:** Add the agentic features (tools, memory, guardrails). Test with real inputs. Submit the checkpoint.
- **Day 6–7:** Deploy publicly (hosting link). Fix bugs. Write README. Record the demo.
- **Day 8 (14–15 Nov):** Final submission, LinkedIn post, community update. **Submit well before 11:45 PM GST on 15 Nov**; do not wait for the last hour.

## Time-zone warning
Deadlines are in **GST (UTC+4)**. If you live elsewhere, convert the deadline and aim to submit at least several hours early to allow for upload problems, platform slowness or lost internet.

## Demo and showcase events (information shared in the challenge materials)
The "Submit for Demo" form is for people who **already have an app** and want to demo it. Listed options in the materials: Dubai AI Festival (25 Oct), JLT Astrolabs (5 Nov), Dubai Future Foundation (6 Nov), and an Online Demo on **10 Nov**. Check the form for which options are still open.
