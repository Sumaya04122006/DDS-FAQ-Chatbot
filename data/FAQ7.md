# Building Agentic AI Application Challenge – November 2026 Edition

## What is this challenge?
The **Building Agentic AI Application Challenge** is a hands-on, online challenge run by **Decoding Data Science (DDS)**. Tagline: **"Launch, Learn, and Lead — in Just 8 Days."**
Participants build, deploy and showcase a real working AI application in 8 days. The November 2026 edition is supported by **DDS and JetBrains**.

## Dates (November 2026 edition)
- **Starts:** 7 November 2026, 9:00 AM GST (Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4)
- **Final submission deadline:** 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM GST (UTC+4)
- Format: **remote / online**. Winners are announced at a DDS community meetup.

## Cost
- The challenge is **free to join** (the page says free "for a limited time").
- Support materials and recordings (Support Kit, Vault) are **optional and paid** (some can be free or discounted; see the Support Materials document).

## Why participate?
- Build and launch your own AI app in 8 days. **No prior experience needed.**
- Gain visibility by showcasing your project to a global AI audience on LinkedIn.
- Receive mentorship, daily guidance and community feedback.
- Win scholarships, credits and licenses from top AI providers.

## What participants get
- **Daily checkpoints:** step-by-step tasks guiding you through building the app.
- **Expert guidance:** tutorials and resources for beginners and intermediates.
- **Real-world skills:** learn to deploy your AI application online and showcase it on LinkedIn.
- **Community support:** a community to share ideas and get feedback.

## Who should join?
- **Aspiring AI developers** who want a portfolio-worthy AI application.
- **Python enthusiasts** who want to apply skills to real-world AI projects.
- **Career switchers** moving into AI and data science roles.
- Complete beginners are supported through the No-Code/Low-Code path.
