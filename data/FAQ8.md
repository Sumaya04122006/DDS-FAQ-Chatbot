# Support Materials, Add-ons and Free Resources

The challenge itself is free. **Support materials and recordings are optional and available for a fee.** Nothing below is required to take part.

## Free resources
- **Challenge application agent:** https://decodingdatascience-challengeapp.hf.space
- **YouTube playlist (free):** https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLkT-YdCzlPEMSunh4V3K_YkyuqSxJUT0I
- **Project Idea and Planning Template** (download on Day 0)
- **AI Builder Codex course:** https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/courses/the-dds-builder-codex
- **Free Python for AI Applications course ($400 value):** join the DDS Skool community, stay active and reach **Level 3** to unlock it. Skool: https://skool.com/decoding-data-science-6929/

## Optional add-ons
### AI Builder's Support Kit (50% off)
- A support kit for the challenge. **Free for anyone who completes all Day 0 checkpoints before the challenge starts.**
- Link (with 50% discount code embedded): https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/courses/ai-app-builder-challenge-support-kit-sep-2026-1?discountCode=SUPPORTCHALLENGE50
- The materials give the free-completion cut-off as "before the challenge starts"; the challenge starts **7 Nov 2026, 9:00 AM GST**. (One slide mentions a different date for the cut-off, so complete Day 0 as early as possible and confirm with the organisers if unsure.)

### AI Builder's Vault (50% off)
- 50+ videos, 100+ hours of content from past workshops, top demos and presentations from earlier scholarship winners, plus access to future challenge materials and recordings.
- Recommended for **serious participants aiming to win**; optional otherwise.
- Discount code printed in the materials: **CHALLENGA50PR**
- Link: https://nas.com/artificialintelligence/digital-files/tqnq?discountCode=CHALLENGA50PR

## Honest advice on paid add-ons
You can complete and win this challenge without paying for anything. Buy the Vault only if you want structured examples and are serious about competing. The free Day 0 route to the Support Kit is worth doing regardless.
